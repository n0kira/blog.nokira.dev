# Where is my username from?

Hi everyone!

So my username hasn't always been "nokira", but I changed it quite a lot of times.
Around 2016/2017, I don't remember exactly, I had to make an account on Roblox since they were removing Guests and so when I signed in, my username should have been "Nikitawilli" which combined both of my dogs' names at the time, Nikita and Willi, but I must have typed something wrong since now the account has the username "Nikirawilli1". Then in the next months/years I lost multiple accounts since I never really remembered my credentials and in 2020 I made another account, which after another typo became "nokirawilli08". This to me felt more unique than the one I had before and so I didn't change it, but most importantly I kept the password safe.

But this username was only on roblox, because on other games and platforms I just used some random names until, in 2025 if I am not wrong, I decided to find a unique username I could use across all platforms so I could always remember it and built some kind of identity around a single username. So I decided to just strip down my roblox username and ended up with "nokira" ! 

Pretty cool right? :)
