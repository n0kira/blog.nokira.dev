# My thoughts about AI

I believe AI is a wonderful tool, it surely has some downsides, like enviromental impact and especially an impact on us people.
What do I mean by that?
For my first point, well I believe everyone knows that about the first part, to build AI we need a massive amount of data centers and they are huge and are replacing nauture.
For the second point, people sadly use AI for literally everything, we went from searching stuff on google, reddit or books, to ask simple questions to AI. At its root, it may seems fine, using a tool that searches the web hundreds of times fuster than any of us, but I believe that by doing that, people are losing some basic skills. Sometimes you see people have simple questions that require one query on a browser, but instead they use AI. This effect is particulary bad when applied to studying. With this I am not saying it shouldn't be used, the problem relies entierly on HOW it's used.
Take for example writing essays or doing homeworks. I know, maybe its not the funniest activity, but people are losing the ability to write simple essays without the help of AI, or they don't know anymore how to study with books and resources.
As I said, I consider it a really great tool, and I am not saying I don't use it. It happens to mee too to use AI for studying, but I use it for explainations of complex topics, or to find resources online in a matter of seconds.

So after all, we should learn how to use AI properly instead of blindly doing stuff, including me.
