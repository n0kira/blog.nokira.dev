# GTWC Endurance Championship!

Yesterday, August 30th the GTWC raced in the Nurburgring for a 3 hours Endurance race. Car #48 started 8th on the grid and to win the Endurance championship they needed at least a 4th position since their opponent (Aston Martin #7), who was directly behind them with only a few points needed to take the first position, was starting first.
After 3 hours of intense racing, the Mamba (car #48) managed to gain some positions after some exciting fights and ended up in 5th position overall. During the last 5 minutes of racing, car #7 was still first, and in 3rd position was McLaren #59 which got a 10 seconds penalty. 

The race comes to an end and with the penalty, car #59 falls behind AND #48 FINISHES RIGHT IN 4TH POSITION TAKING AT HOME THE ENDURANCE CHAMPIONSHIP WINNER TITLE.

AHHH THAT WAS AN EPIC RACE FROM EVERYONE BUT I'M SO HAPPY FOR MY AUER, ENGEL AND STOLZ :D
